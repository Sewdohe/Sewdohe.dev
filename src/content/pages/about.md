👋 Hello 👋

My name is Josh. I'm more proudly known as **Sewdohe** online. That name's not nearly so average as *Josh* 🤮 

I've had a hobby of piddling with various forms of computing since, well, I've had a computer with a reliable internet connection 😆 I got my start with an ugly brushed-metal background with gaudy red text web-page when I was around 12 - and I fell in love 💗

I've been hooked to computers ever since. While I didn't get seriously into development until around the age of 18, I have always loved being on my PC, up until this very day at which point I am 32 years old. My love for programming and computing has honestly only **grown** since that time. My skills have matured greatly over the years, the library of languages I can write has turned into an infinite list as my understanding of general programming has blossomed. I am to the point now that I can write nearly anything in any language if I put my mind to it hard enough.

These days I work as a mechanic, sadly. 😖

A serious of lax decisions in life and screwing about at dead-end jobs has resulted in my love of computing to be but a hobby. But alas, I am ready to change that! It's time that I get into tech as a full-time job.

My current plan is to get away from being a flag-rate mechanic first to relieve some stress from my life - at which point my next step once I get more stable income will be to attend school and get a degree for Computer Science. It's where I belong in this world - and I know it. I will get there 💪
