## Shit has hit the fan 💩

I was watching a video from a YouTuber that I've never seen before, [Big Tug](https://youtube.com/@biggtugg)...and WOW has this man hit on something that is bothering me. I mean...I knew America is on a sharp decline but **wow** these companies are getting away with egregious shit nowadays. I guess this one in particular hit home for me because in either direction from my house, less than a mile away, is a Dollar General and a Family Dollar. "Dollar" stores. So they call them.

Now, I do remember when things in a dollar store...well...costs a fucking dollar. Thaaaaaat is no longer the case in these stores. Inflation has reached all time highs. Our politicians are all blatantly corrupt. Our laws are defined by the highest bidding corporation. Ice agents parade around the country murdering Americans. National guard enacted on our own citizens.

It's truly a shameful time to call America my home - patriotism be damned. I am ashamed to be from this country. The thought of what other people in the world must think of us makes me shudder. If you are from another country reading this - please know that we aren't all as the media portrays us.

Misinformation and propaganda run rampant. AI "troll farms" dictate much of the narrative now. Pitting people against each other over whatever latest tragic topic was thrust into the media. Fake AI slop videos make it difficult to see what is real and what is fake. It's a page taken from the Russian handbook. They want us at each others' throats. They want us so confused what is real and fake that we just don't care anymore. It's working. I'm exhausted.

I'm stuck in a dead-end job. I have no extra money for recreation. I can't have time to relax. Me and my wife work opposing schedules to make ends (barely) meet. We can't spend time together. I can't buy my kids nice things anymore. I just work - and eat - and sleep. Occasionally play a video game on my Steam Deck.
